# Ideas Inbox

Drop raw ideas, bugs, and friction here — anytime, in any format. One per bullet is
ideal but not required. The weekly loop runner ingests everything below the marker,
turns each into a scored backlog item in `BACKLOG.json`, and moves the processed text
into the "Processed" section (so nothing is lost and nothing is double-counted).

You can add ideas three ways:
1. Type a bullet under **Unprocessed** below.
2. Use the **Add idea** box in the dashboard (sends it to a chat session).
3. Just tell Claude in chat: "add to the life-stages inbox: ..."
